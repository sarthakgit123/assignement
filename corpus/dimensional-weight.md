# Dimensional Weight Calculation

Billable weight is the greater of actual scale weight and dimensional weight.

Dimensional weight = (Length x Width x Height, in inches) / DIM divisor

DIM divisors:

- **Domestic** (US 48 contiguous states): **139**
- **Alaska, Hawaii, and Puerto Rico**: **139**
- **International** (all cross-border, including Canada and Mexico): **166**

Round each dimension to the nearest whole inch before multiplying. Round the
final dimensional weight up to the next whole pound.
