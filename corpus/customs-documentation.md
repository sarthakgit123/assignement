# International Customs Documentation

Every cross-border shipment requires, at tender:

- Commercial invoice, three copies, showing per-line HS code, country of origin,
  and declared value in USD
- Packing list matching the commercial invoice line for line
- Certificate of origin, where a trade agreement rate is claimed
- Importer of record name, address, and tax ID
- Power of attorney on file with Meridian's customs broker, valid for the
  importer of record

Shipments tendered with incomplete documentation are held at the gateway and not
loaded. Support notifies the shipper the same business day, and the shipment
remains at the gateway until documents are corrected.

If a shipment is refused by the destination customs authority, the broker files a
notice of refusal and the shipment is held in bonded storage pending instruction
from the importer of record. Bonded storage accrues daily from the date of
refusal.
