# Proof of Delivery Requirements

**Standard service.** The driver captures a delivery scan plus a photo of the
freight at the delivery point. No signature required.

**Signature Required service.** The driver must capture a legible printed name, a
signature, and the signer's relationship to the consignee (employee, agent, or
resident). A delivery scan alone is not proof of delivery on a Signature Required
shipment. A claim on such a shipment is adjudicated in the customer's favor if
the signature record is missing or illegible.

**Adult Signature Required service.** As above, plus a government-issued photo ID
check with the ID type and last four characters recorded. Meridian does not store
ID images.

POD records are retained for 18 months and are retrievable by the customer
through the portal. For shipments older than 18 months, Support can retrieve a
record only if it was archived under a litigation hold.
