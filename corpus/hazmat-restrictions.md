# Hazardous Materials Restrictions

Meridian accepts limited-quantity hazmat on ground service only, under a signed
hazmat addendum. The following are never accepted on any service:

- Explosives (Class 1, all divisions)
- Radioactive materials (Class 7)
- Infectious substances (Division 6.2)
- Bulk compressed gases above 1,000 L water capacity
- Standalone lithium-ion cells rated above 100 Wh

Lithium-ion batteries **installed in equipment** are accepted on ground service,
limited to two shipping units per package, with UN3481 marking. Standalone
lithium-ion cells at or below 100 Wh are accepted on ground service under the
hazmat addendum, maximum 8 cells per package.

Air service accepts no lithium-ion in any configuration.
