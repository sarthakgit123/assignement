# Incident Escalation Policy (v2) -- DEPRECATED

Effective: 2024-03-01
Status: Superseded on 2026-01-15 by Incident Escalation Policy v3. Retained for
audit reference only. Do not apply to incidents opened on or after 2026-01-15.
Owner: Network Operations

**Tier 1 -- Routine.** Single-shipment delays, address corrections, minor
documentation errors. Handled by the assigned Support Associate.
Response SLA: 12 business hours.

**Tier 2 -- Elevated.** Multi-shipment delays, or any incident with declared value
above $25,000. Routed to the Duty Supervisor. Response SLA: 8 business hours.

**Tier 3 -- Critical.** Facility outage, hazmat release, or injury. Routed to the
Regional Operations Director. Response SLA: 2 hours during business hours.
