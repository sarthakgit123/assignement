# Incident Escalation Policy (v3)

Effective: 2026-01-15
Supersedes: Incident Escalation Policy v2 (2024-03-01)
Owner: Network Operations

Meridian classifies operational incidents into three tiers.

**Tier 1 -- Routine.** Single-shipment delays under 24 hours, address corrections,
minor documentation errors. Handled by the assigned Support Associate.
Response SLA: 8 business hours. Resolution target: 2 business days.

**Tier 2 -- Elevated.** Multi-shipment delays, temperature excursions on
refrigerated freight, a customer's second Tier 1 incident within 30 days, or any
incident with declared value above $10,000. Routed to the Duty Supervisor.
Response SLA: 4 business hours. Resolution target: 1 business day.

**Tier 3 -- Critical.** Full facility outage, hazmat release, injury, regulatory
hold, or loss of a full trailer. Routed immediately to the Regional Operations
Director and the on-call executive. Response SLA: 30 minutes, 24/7. No resolution
target -- Tier 3 incidents run until closed by the Director.

Escalation is one-directional. An incident may be raised to a higher tier at any
time, but only the Regional Operations Director may lower an incident's tier.
