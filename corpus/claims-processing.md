# Claims Processing and Routing

Once a claim is confirmed eligible under the Claim and Refund Eligibility policy,
it is routed by claimed value:

- **Under $500** -- auto-adjudicated. Payout issued within 5 business days.
- **$500 to $4,999.99** -- reviewed by a Claims Associate. Decision within
  10 business days.
- **$5,000 and above** -- escalated to the Regional Claims Desk. These require an
  independent damage survey before adjudication and have no fixed decision window.

Routing is based on the *claimed* value, not the adjudicated payout. A claim that
is reduced on adjudication stays with the desk that received it.

Eligibility is always determined before routing. An ineligible claim is never
routed to any desk, regardless of its claimed value.
