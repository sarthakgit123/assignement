# Carrier Performance Rating Scale

Meridian rates every contracted line-haul carrier on a four-band scale, reviewed
quarterly. The bands are written in Roman numerals to distinguish them from the
Arabic-numeral incident tiers used by Support.

**Tier I** -- On-time delivery at or above 97%, claims rate under 0.2%. Eligible
for guaranteed-capacity contracts and priority tender.

**Tier II** -- On-time delivery 92% to 96.9%, claims rate under 0.5%. Standard
tender rotation. A Tier II carrier may not be tendered temperature-controlled or
high-value freight without Procurement sign-off.

**Tier III** -- On-time delivery 85% to 91.9%. Overflow tender only.

**Tier IV** -- Below 85% on-time delivery, or any single quarter with a claims
rate above 1.5%. Removed from the tender pool pending a remediation plan.

Carrier ratings are unrelated to incident escalation tiers. A Tier II carrier
rating describes contract standing, not incident severity.
