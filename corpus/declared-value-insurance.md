# Declared Value and Carrier Liability

Every shipment carries Meridian's default carrier liability of **$100 per
shipment**, regardless of actual value, unless a higher value is declared at the
time of tender.

Declared value is elected per shipment on the bill of lading and is charged at
**$0.85 per $100 of declared value**, minimum charge $12.

Maximum declared value per shipment is $100,000. Above that, the shipper must
arrange third-party cargo insurance. Meridian will tender the freight, but its
own liability remains capped at $100,000.

Declared value is not insurance. It raises Meridian's liability ceiling; it does
not guarantee a payout. A claim on a shipment with declared value is still
adjudicated under the claims policy, and a claim that fails eligibility pays
nothing regardless of declared value.

Shipments tendered under a released value waiver carry liability of $0.50 per
pound and are ineligible for claims.
