# SLA Definitions

These terms have specific meanings in Meridian policy. Use them precisely.

**Response time.** The interval between an incident being logged and the assigned
owner making first substantive contact with the customer. An automated
acknowledgement email does not stop the response clock. Response SLAs are stated
per incident tier in the escalation policy.

**Resolution time.** The interval between an incident being logged and the
incident being closed, meaning the underlying issue is corrected and the customer
has confirmed, or three business days have passed without objection. Resolution
targets are targets, not commitments, and are not credit-bearing.

**Business hours.** 08:00 to 18:00 local to the assigned owner's facility, Monday
through Friday, excluding observed holidays. A 4-business-hour SLA logged at
17:00 on a Friday is therefore due at 12:00 the following Monday.

**Credit-bearing.** An SLA whose breach entitles the customer to a service credit
under their contract. Only the Tier 3 response SLA is credit-bearing.
