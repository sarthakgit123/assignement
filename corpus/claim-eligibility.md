# Claim and Refund Eligibility

A customer may file a damage, loss, or shortage claim against a shipment if all
of the following hold:

1. The claim is filed within **21 calendar days** of the delivery scan, or, for a
   lost shipment, within 21 calendar days of the scheduled delivery date.
2. Photographic evidence is attached showing both the packaging and the contents.
   Concealed-damage claims additionally require a photo of the outer carton taken
   before it was opened.
3. The shipment was not tendered under a released value waiver.

Claims filed after day 21 are ineligible regardless of merit and are closed
without review. There is no appeal path for a late filing. The customer's only
remedy is a goodwill credit at the discretion of the Account Manager, which is
not a claim payout and does not draw on carrier liability.

Eligible claims proceed to Claims Processing for routing and adjudication.
