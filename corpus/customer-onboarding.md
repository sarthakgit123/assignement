# New Customer Onboarding

Onboarding runs five business days from signed agreement to first live tender.

1. **Day 1 -- Account creation.** Legal entity name, billing address, and
   remit-to details are entered by the Account Manager.
2. **Day 1-2 -- Credit review.** Finance submits the customer to the third-party
   credit bureau and records the returned score on the account. Accounts that
   fail credit review are placed on prepay terms and may still ship. Accounts
   that pass are assigned payment terms and a credit limit by Finance.
3. **Day 3 -- Rate table load.** Contracted rates and the applicable fuel
   surcharge zone map are loaded.
4. **Day 4 -- Systems handoff.** EDI or API credentials issued; test tender
   exchanged.
5. **Day 5 -- First live tender.**

Credit limits, payment terms, and credit-review scoring thresholds are set by
Finance and are not published in this handbook. Support should direct any
customer question about a specific credit limit to that customer's Account
Manager.
